# Language and calculations

## Establish the inputs

Identify the exact test and variant, test date, intended program, and scores explicitly labelled **Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking**. Do not infer order from an unlabelled series of numbers. Distinguish IELTS General Training from Academic and retakes, CELPIP-General from LS, PTE Core from Academic, and the relevant French test versions.

If the user only needs a score conversion, convert the supplied, labelled components when possible; do not hold useful partial work for an unnecessary full intake. State that incomplete components cannot establish an all-abilities requirement. Ask for the program/date only when test acceptance, validity, score scale, or applicability depends on it.

## Convert using the applicable official table

1. Retrieve IRCC's current equivalency guidance and any program-specific instructions. Use the exact variant and relevant score-report scale. Do not rely on a frozen chart bundled with this skill.
2. For TEF Canada, verify the test date, report labels, score scale, and the score column required by the intended application system. Do not assume the report's newest scale is the system's required entry scale.
3. Map each labelled ability independently against the published thresholds or inclusive ranges. Do not average four abilities to determine whether each meets a minimum. Do not round up, interpolate, swap columns, or extrapolate beyond a published chart.
4. Validate that scores are possible for the specified test, including permitted increments and scale limits. If a component falls below the chart's lowest published level, state that rather than inventing a lower equivalency.
5. Preserve the table's top-level treatment, including “and above” where applicable; do not manufacture precision beyond the chart.
6. Separate the conversion result from whether the test variant, date, and results satisfy the intended program's requirements.

## CRS, selection points, fees, and other arithmetic

- Obtain the current official scoring rules or calculator appropriate to the program and date. Establish every input that changes the result; use a clearly labelled scenario/range if inputs are missing.
- Distinguish Express Entry CRS, program eligibility grids, provincial rankings, and language conversion. Do not treat them as interchangeable scores.
- Include spouse configuration, age at the relevant date, valid education/ECA, labelled language results, qualifying work periods, nomination, and any additional factors only as applicable under the verified rules.
- Do not reuse historical point awards after a rule change. Show the category breakdown and assumptions, calculate with available deterministic tooling when useful, and cross-check against the official method.
- Avoid double-counting overlapping experience or exceeding category caps. Verify treatment of part-time work, student work, self-employment, authorization, and foreign/Canadian work for the exact program.
- For fees, use the official fee list and separate government charges from any user-supplied service fee and applicable tax assumptions.

## Dates and deadlines

Identify the triggering event, governing provision, notification/service method, location classification if relevant, counting rule, timezone, holidays, and extension exceptions. Verify the rule before doing arithmetic. Show the trigger and assumptions with any calculated deadline, and identify unresolved facts that could change it. Do not silently treat calendar days as business days or infer that another request pauses time.

Use only synthetic or minimally necessary inputs in calculators or execution tools; do not embed client identifiers in scripts or public queries. If the official table or rule cannot be verified, provide the method and missing verification instead of a definitive legal or eligibility result.
