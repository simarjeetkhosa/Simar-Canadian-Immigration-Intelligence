# Client and public drafts

## Choose audience and purpose

Use the requested format: concise client email, explanation, document request, consultation summary, Punjabi post, public update, or submission draft. Reuse established facts and tone. Ask for brand/signatory details only when needed for the deliverable; do not insert unrelated personal or business history.

For a simple rewrite or translation with no legal claim added or adopted, preserve the supplied meaning and do not launch unnecessary research. If the draft states legal advice, current policy, eligibility, a deadline, or a promise, verify it or identify the claim as needing verification. Do not make a doubtful claim sound authoritative through polishing.

## English and Punjabi

- Use clear professional English or natural Punjabi in Gurmukhi, matching the user's request. Use Roman Punjabi only on request.
- Preserve conditional wording: “may qualify” must not become “will qualify.” Preserve negations, dates, amounts, citations, and eligibility conditions precisely.
- Keep official program names, IRCC/PGWP/SOWP/CLB abbreviations, and form identifiers recognizable; briefly explain them in the chosen language when useful.
- Preserve source links. Do not claim a Punjabi translation is an official government translation or a certified translation.
- For bilingual output, keep both versions substantively equivalent and visually separate.

## Client communications

Lead with the result or required action. Explain the reason briefly and provide an actionable document list or next step. State a deadline only when its basis is established. Keep internal uncertainty notes separate from the copy-ready draft while retaining material qualifications in the client-facing wording.

Use placeholders for unknown names, dates, fees, appointment links, and signatories. Include a professional registration number only when the correct signatory and details are supplied or verified. Do not imply that Simar holds another person's credentials.

If the user requests an AMNI-branded communication, use that brand in that draft. Do not treat prior office prices, response times, locations, service policies, contact details, or offers as permanent defaults; use the current task's confirmed terms.

## Public immigration updates

Verify the official announcement and operative instructions. Separate an announcement from implementation and identify who is included/excluded, the effective date, transitional treatment, and practical action.

For a short social post, retain the conditions that materially change eligibility. Include a compact source or provide a source note alongside the post when the format requires it. Do not invent urgency, “guaranteed approval,” exclusive government access, or a success rate. Do not add a paid consultation offer unless requested.

## Draft formats

- **Client update:** Main point → relevance to this client → required action/documents → verified deadline if applicable → requested sign-off.
- **Public update:** What changed → affected group → effective date → key exception → practical next step/source.
- **Consultation summary:** Supplied facts → conditional assessment → evidence gaps → options and next steps.
- **Submission:** Requested outcome → supported facts and chronology → applicable rule → evidence-linked response → appropriate requested remedy.

For a requested standalone document, use the relevant document-creation workflow and inspect the final artifact. For a short draft in chat, use the supported writing presentation where available. Sending, posting, signing, or filing is a separate action governed by the user's authorization; drafting alone does not authorize it.
