# Professional use and portability

## Adapt to the person and forum

Use the user's stated role without inventing a licence, firm, retainer, delegation or signature. Ask about jurisdiction, scope or responsible reviewer only when it affects the requested action. Do not block ordinary research and drafting on an unnecessary credentials questionnaire.

| Role or work | Deliverable and review boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Immigration lawyer | Evidence-linked advice draft, submissions, litigation research and JR record/grounds audit; counsel decides legal strategy and approves filings. |
| RCIC | Program analysis, factual evidence gaps, NOC assessment, permit and PR drafts, refusal triage and referral materials; verify current licence class and forum authority before representative work, including IRB work. |
| Licensed paralegal | Research and case preparation within the applicable regulator's permitted scope, jurisdiction and forum; do not assume a paralegal title authorizes every immigration service or Federal Court practice. |
| Staff, students or unlicensed assistants | Administrative preparation, evidence organization and drafts for the responsible authorized professional; do not represent the assistant as independently authorized. |
| Self-represented applicant | Clear information, sourced research and requested drafts; do not invent a representative relationship. |

For Federal Court JR, separate counsel-facing research/referral preparation from legal representation or advice by a non-lawyer. Check the current Court representation rules and any actual court order. A professional title alone, or an MIT licence, does not authorize Federal Court practice. Continue record organization and urgent issue identification while identifying the appropriate reviewer.

Use current primary sources before making a concrete scope conclusion:

These are source entry points checked on 2026-10-28, not a substitute for checking the applicable current rules and regulator's scope.

## Prepare useful professional work product

For a case audit, record: case alias; program/forum; assessment date; relevant event and notification dates; source/record inventory; evidence-linked chronology; requirements and gaps; risk level with reason; verified authorities and adverse treatment; unresolved questions; owner and next action. Distinguish an internal draft from an approved version. Do not set an artificial percentage probability of success.

For a requested form draft, retain the source-to-field mapping and draft hash/version. Supply a review list for identity, history, dates, declarations, missing fields, representative details and rendering/barcode validation when applicable. The human reviewer must inspect the exact version. Record their actual review and decision only after receiving it; never pre-populate a completed approval, attestation or signature. A materially changed draft needs renewed review of the changes and final version.

Use anonymized examples for public demos and searches. Client records remain separate from distributable skills and public MCP workflow resources. Do not infer client consent from installation. Follow the firm's established access, retention and client confidentiality requirements within the authorized work.

## Work across AI hosts

Resolve scripts and references relative to this SKILL.md, not an absolute path from another computer. Use the host's documented skill loader or an explicitly loaded instruction/knowledge file. Do not claim a plain file attachment has installed an executable skill.

Check these capabilities only as needed: primary-source web retrieval; readable attachments; Python and PDF dependencies; available Adobe operations; persistent case artifacts; scheduler; and MCP transport. If a capability is absent, provide the available research plan, field mapping or draft and state the specific unfinished step. Do not invent equivalent tools or silently lower verification requirements.

Treat Adobe Acrobat as an optional application integration. Installation of Acrobat does not prove the current AI connector supports interactive form filling or XFA validation. Discover actual tools; prefer a capable connected tool and use the PDF workflow's documented fallback when insufficient. Preserve human review on every host.

The optional Simar MCP server exposes bundled workflow documents and templates through read-only tools. It is not a live immigration database, legal search engine, scheduler, Adobe remote controller, or case filing service. Tool responses are workflow context, never verified legal authority. Use the host's research tools to retrieve and verify current official material and actual judgments.

For another AI host, do not transfer passwords, tokens, private client records, scheduled jobs, platform permissions or account connections as part of installation. Configure those separately through the target platform's supported mechanisms. Reuse the companion Monitor skill only when the host has a real scheduler and the user requests a task.
