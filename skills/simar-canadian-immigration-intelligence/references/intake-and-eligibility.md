# Intake and eligibility

## Check urgency before broad research

For a live status, refusal, fairness-letter, appeal, or removal issue, extract the relevant notice and notification dates, status expiry, proposed action date, and any stated deadline. Verify the current governing rule and procedural route before calculating a date.

Do not infer that a matter legally arose inside or outside Canada solely from where the applicant is today. Do not assume that a reconsideration request, new application, information request, or representative contact suspends another deadline or creates work/study authorization. Verify each effect separately.

If a deadline may be imminent, say so immediately, identify what must be verified, and recommend prompt review by the appropriate authorized professional. Continue the evidence review; do not make collecting GCMS notes a prerequisite to recognizing urgency. Distinguish court representation from immigration consulting, and verify applicable representation rules when relevant.

## Collect only decision-changing facts

Use facts already available. Request the relevant subset:

| Issue | Material facts to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Temporary status/work/study | Location, status document and conditions, expiry, filing/receipt dates, decision notices, travel, exact permit category, planned activity |
| PGWP/study pathway | Institution/program details, study and completion dates, delivery/status history, permit/application dates, relevant language/field requirements |
| Spousal/family permits | Relationship evidence, principal applicant's exact category/status, relevant study/work details, expiry, application date |
| Economic/PNP pathway | Program and province, age at relevant date, education/ECA, labelled language scores/date, duties and dates of work, authorization, hours, nomination/offer facts |
| Sponsorship/citizenship | Relevant category, status, family/presence history and dates, prior applications, specific possible eligibility bar |
| Refusal/fairness issue | Actual letter and reasons, notification date, submitted record, GCMS if available, previous decisions, requested remedy |

Do not ask for sensitive identifiers when an anonymized timeline or relevant excerpt will answer the question. Do not equate job title with NOC/TEER: assess actual duties against the official classification and identify uncertainty.

## Build an assessment

1. Establish the exact stream and applicable dates, including transitional rules.
2. Verify that the program or intake is operational for the proposed action. Separate eligibility criteria from openings, invitations, caps, and quotas.
3. Map each decisive requirement to supplied evidence. Separate mandatory requirements from discretionary assessment and supporting evidence.
4. Keep temporary resident status, a travel visa, permission to enter, and authorization to work/study distinct.
5. State what is met, unmet, or unknown. If recommending a pathway, explain the decisive reason, evidence gaps, and next step.

Do not infer a final eligibility result from one favourable factor or a draw score. Do not imply an application cures expired status or authorizes work without verifying the governing rule and facts.

## Return the appropriate level of detail

For a quick screening, give a conditional conclusion, two or three decisive factors, and the smallest useful set of questions. For a detailed review, give a dated timeline, the requirement/evidence matrix, and a prioritized action list.

For document checklists, distinguish official required items, items required only in the applicant's circumstances, and recommended supporting evidence. Verify form versions and country/stream instructions when the task depends on them. Do not invent a “mandatory” document from customary office practice.
